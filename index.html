How I Approach Technical Ownership

Difficult projects rarely fail because of one isolated component. They fail at interfaces, assumptions, and untested interactions.

Define the real problemClarify the actual need, constraints, risks, and success criteria before committing to an architecture.
Build to learnUse prototypes, fixtures, tests, and instrumentation to replace assumptions with evidence.
Trace failures across interfacesInvestigate mechanical, optical, electrical, thermal, software, manufacturing, and human factors together.
Commission the full systemStay engaged through integration, validation, deployment, and the final details required for reliable operation.
